Hi, I am Marco from Italy.  

I am a data scientist with broad knowledge in time series analysis, forecasting, 
anomaly detection, and econometrics. 
I specialized in developing and enhancing business forecasting processes and practices. 
Over the years I developed and contributed to core forecasting systems in sectors 
like e-commerce fashion, airline, tourism and travel. I also guided and mentored 
junior colleagues in developing predictive models that enable accurate forecasting. 
I am passionate about advancing the field of forecasting and fostering collaboration 
between academia and industry. I do academic work in time series forecasting, and 
I am a Member of the International Institute of Forecasters. 
I also hold multiple certifications related to the analysis and forecasting of 
time series data. Recently I have enjoyed teaching professionals and university 
students about time series analysis, machine learning, programming, and statistics.  

I speak Italian, English, and a little bit of French.  

Have a look at my [Curriculum Vitae](https://marcozanotti.github.io/CVitae/cv_resume/cv_en.html)!  


## Professional Experience

I am a **Data Scientist** with **10 years** of working experience.

I started to work as Junior Data Scientist in 2017, in an Italian
multinational tourism company, [Uvet AMEX GBT](https://www.uvetgbt.com/), 
and its airline company Blue Panorama. 
There, I learnt a ton of things related to **programming in production** and I 
had the opportunity to work with a lot of different data related to business travel, 
pharmaceutical events, online bookings, hotels' reservations, and proprietary 
airline data.

In 2020, I started to work as a Data Scientist for T-Voice, a start-up
company of the [Triboo Group](https://triboo.com/), where I
worked as a Senior Consultant to develop client specific data analysis solutions, 
and I was responsible for the **coordination of the R&D Team**.

In 2021, following an M&A operation, I became a Data Scientist in 
[Blogmeter](https://www.blogmeter.it/it/home), a company of the 
[Shibumi Group](https://shibumi.group/). There I helped to integrate AI solutions 
into the main applications of the company and I was responsible for the
**coordination of the Product Team**.

In 2024 I moved to [Wanan Luxury](https://www.wananluxury.com/) as a Lead
Data Scientist - Forecasting Specialist. There I **led the forecasting team**, 
developing demand forecasting solutions for thousands of e-commerce 
fashion products (SKUs), and training junior collegues.

<!-- In 2026 I had the great opportunity to work as a Senior Researcher for 
[iqast: Intelligent Forecasting](http://iqast.de/), an important German company 
in the field of forecasting softwares.   -->

Throughout all my working experience I was responsible for the
development of **time series forecasting** models, both for internal and
clients needs.
I developed and maintained diverse statistical and machine learning models to
predict:  
- product demand at different aggregation levels to support both operational and 
strategic decisions,  
- daily inbound calls to optimize scheduling hours,  
- air passenger demand over several destinations to support aircraft planning, 
- business car rental demand to support fleets management, etc.  

I coordinated and contributed to the development of forecasting tools to predict 
future demand for tens of thousands item-level (SKUs) time series across several 
markets worldwide, and with different product hierarchies. 
I also helped to integrate probabilistic methods to support more informed decisions. 
Moreover, I successfully improved the approach to forecasting, resulting in a 
significant reduction of errors and facilitating the faster adoption of machine 
learning solutions by the business. 
Finally, I provided mentorship to junior colleagues, supporting their professional 
growth and development in the field of forecasting.  

I have also been a **Ph.D. Researcher in Statistics** at the University
of Milano Bicocca, where I worked on academic research projects related to
time series forecasting.

## Publications

I actively work on academic research projects related to **time series forecasting**.
In particular, I published few articles that focus on sustainable forecasting 
practices and the development of high-performance forecasting systems.

- [Zanotti, M. (2025). On the retraining frequency of global models in retail demand forecasting. Machine Learning with Applications, 22, 100769.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mlwa.2025.100769)  
- [Zanotti, M. (2025). The cost of ensembling: is it always worth combining? arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04677)  
- [Zanotti, M. (2026). Analyzing the retraining frequency of global forecasting models: towards more stable forecasting systems. arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05776)  
- Zanotti, M., Zangirolami, V., Pavesi, F. (2026). An evaluation of ensemble strategies for time series anomaly detection. Springer  
- Zanotti, M. (2027). Sustainable forecasting with artificial intelligence: The role of retraining frequency. In M. Hamoudia, E. Spiliotis, & P. Montero-Manso (Eds.), New frontiers in forecasting with artificial intelligence: Methods and applications. Palgrave Macmillan  

I also contributed in several researches in other fields helping with the formalization
and implementation of statistical methods.

- Pavesi, F., Zanotti, M., Zangirolami, V. (2026). A characterization of Gaussian processes over flat tori. Springer  
- Zangirolami, V., Pavesi, F., Zanotti, M. (2026). Safe Exogenous State Reinforcement Learning for water tank system. Springer  
- Mazzucchelli, L., Zanotti, M., et al. (2026). Do Short Exposure and Systematic Risk Exposure Drive Asymmetries in the Disposition Effect? arXiv
- Pierfederici, M. E., et al. (2026), Climate changing's effects on Silene acaulis. arXiv

In 2021 I also published an **R package** related to the topic of behavioral 
finance. It is the first R package allowing R users to perform several types of 
behavioral analysis.  

- [dispositionEffect, CRAN](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=dispositionEffect)  

The software has been presented at the [useR!2021 Conference](https://user2021.r-project.org/) 
and it has been listed on the [Top 40 New CRAN Packaes](https://rviews.rstudio.com/2021/09/27/august-2021-top-40-new-cran-packages/)
on August 2021.
